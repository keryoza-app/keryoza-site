1. Who we are

Keryoza is an app that turns play into movement and discovery for children.

2. The camera

The game uses the camera only to understand the shape of the room, so it can place things in it.

  • Everything happens inside the phone.
  • We never take photos or videos, and nothing is saved or sent.
  • The camera stops when the game is closed.
  • On Android, this application runs on Google Play Services for AR (ARCore), which is provided by Google LLC and governed by the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Your child's information

Your child's name, age and favourite colour are saved on this phone only and are never sent to us. Children cannot write, chat or contact anyone in Keryoza.

4. Anonymous usage data

We collect general data about how the app is used, for example:

  • which games are played, for how long, and how much the child moved
  • the child's progress in the game and a general age group (never the name or the exact age)
  • crashes and technical problems
  • the phone type, its language, and the approximate country
  • your answers in the parent pages (such as interest in a paid version)
  • an anonymous app ID

This data carries no name and cannot tell us who you are. We use it only to improve Keryoza, never for ads.

We never collect: names, photos, videos, voice, precise location, contacts, or anything a child types.

If you email us or send us the survey, we use your message and your email address only to reply to you and to improve Keryoza.

5. Your choices

Usage data starts when you accept the terms, and you can switch it off at any time.

  • Stop sending: in the parent settings, open "Privacy and data" and switch off "Usage data".
  • Delete usage data: in the parent settings, tap "Delete usage data". Sending stops and anything not sent yet is deleted.
  • Delete everything: in the parent settings, tap "Delete all children's data". Everything on the phone is deleted and sending stops. Anything already sent is deleted automatically within 3 months.

6. Where it is kept

The data is kept on Google's servers (Firebase), which may be outside your country. It is deleted automatically within 3 months at most. We keep only overall numbers (such as how many families played today), which cannot point to anyone.

7. No ads, no selling

Keryoza shows no ads to children, sells no data, shares no data with advertisers, and uses no tracking tools.

8. Keeping data safe

Data is sent encrypted, and only the Keryoza team can access it. Collecting as little as possible is our best protection.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in an important way, the app will show you the new version before it applies.

10. Your rights

You can always switch the data off or delete it, as in section 5. For any question, write to privacy@keryoza.com.
