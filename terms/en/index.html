1. Our agreement

Keryoza is an app for children, played under the supervision of an adult. These terms are an agreement between Keryoza and you, the parent or guardian. By using the app you accept them and confirm that you are an adult aged 18 or over.

2. What Keryoza is

An augmented reality (AR) game app: the child sees virtual things in the space around them and moves to find them, guided by a voice.

3. Safety first

Because the child moves while looking at a screen:

  • The child always plays with an adult present.
  • Play in a safe place indoors, away from stairs, glass and sharp things.
  • Do not play near roads, water or balconies, or in public places.
  • If the child has a health condition affected by screens or movement, such as photosensitive epilepsy or balance problems, ask a doctor first.
  • You are responsible for the child's safety and the play space while playing.

4. The camera

The camera works inside the phone only. We never take photos or videos, and nothing is saved or sent.

5. Augmented reality

  • Keryoza shows virtual things on the screen, and sometimes a whole virtual world instead of the picture of the room. They are not really there, and the screen does not always show what is around the child, so remind your child to watch the real space around them.
  • Augmented reality works only on phones that support it. On Android it needs Google Play Services for AR (ARCore).
  • How exact it is can change with the phone, the light and the place.
