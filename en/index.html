Why Keryoza?

A screen, done differently

Studies show that a lot of sitting in front of screens in young children is linked with weaker focus and language and less sleep, and that an interactive screen is a different thing. That is why we made Keryoza.

Balanced play

We play, then we rest

  1. Game time

    A short game where your child walks, searches and discovers, for the time you choose.

    Best played in a clear, open space with a grown-up nearby.

  2. Time to move

    After every game: time to move away from the screen, and a real-world activity a parent picks.

  3. Kio sleeps

    Kio celebrates, then quietly falls asleep. No countdown, no pressure, no messages calling your child to play.

  4. Parents decide

    Only a parent can open the next game. The daily play limit is the same for everyone, free or paid, and is never for sale.

Keryoza features

A safe learning game that gives your child a lot

  • No ads, ever

    No ads and no pop-ups inside the game.

  • No alerts calling your child

    We send your child no notifications, and nothing is designed to pull them to the screen.

  • AR stays on the phone

    The AR view is processed on the phone only. Nothing of it is recorded, stored or sent.

  • Balanced play with rest

    You choose the game time, then Kio rests. The next game waits for your OK.

  • Movement after every game

    When the game ends, it's time to move away from the screen.

  • Real skills

    Moving, listening, place words, remembering and sharing.

  • In Arabic

    The app is in Arabic, because we believe in the value of the Arabic language and in keeping it alive.

  • Truly fun

    Search-and-discover adventures with a friend who celebrates every find.

  • Parents in control

    Settings and play time sit behind a gate only parents can open. Your child plays; you decide.

What is Keryoza?

Keryoza in short

Keryoza is a children's app for smartphones. It offers interactive educational games in augmented reality (AR) and speaks only Arabic. Kio, a play friend, guides the child, who moves, searches, explores and learns something new in every game. It is made for parents who want a different screen for their children, not less screen. There are no ads, AR runs on the phone only, and no video or picture is recorded or saved.

What is Keryoza?

Keryoza is an app of interactive educational games for children in augmented reality, in Arabic. Kio, a play friend, guides the child as they move, search and discover.

Which phones does Keryoza work on?

Keryoza works on smartphones that support augmented reality (AR). It starts on Android and is coming soon to iPhone.

Is Keryoza free?

Yes, Keryoza is free to download.

Is Keryoza safe for my child?

Keryoza has no ads and no notifications that pull the child back to play. AR runs on the phone only, and no video or picture is recorded or saved.

What does a child learn in Keryoza?

Every game teaches the child one new, clear thing. They move, search and notice details to find what Kio points them to, and every new game brings a new world to discover.

Is Keryoza in Arabic?

Yes, Keryoza speaks only Arabic, in both voice and text. The child hears Kio guiding them step by step.
